# BGM_Campain_Data_Analysis
# Marketing Campaign Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer interaction and sales data across different marketing campaigns and acquisition channels. Using **500 customer interaction records**, the analysis evaluates campaign effectiveness, channel performance, and user engagement to identify key revenue drivers.

## 📁 Dataset Description
The dataset contains **500 rows** and **7 columns** tracking individual customer sessions:
* **Interaction ID:** Unique identifier for each customer session (`INT0001` – `INT0500`).
* **Campaign Type:** Marketing campaign variant (`A` or `B`).
* **Channel:** Traffic source (`Email`, `Instagram`, or `Web Banner`).
* **Customer Type:** User segment (`New` or `Existing`).
* **Converted:** Binary indicator of purchase completion (`1` = Yes, `0` = No).
* **Time on Site (seconds):** Total duration spent on the website during the session.
* **Sales ($):** Revenue generated from the interaction.

## 📈 Key Insights & Findings
* **Total Revenue:** The campaigns generated **$14,659.60** in total sales across all channels.
* **Campaign Performance:** **Campaign B** outperformed Campaign A, driving **59.96%** ($8,790.04) of total revenue compared to **40.04%** ($5,869.56) for Campaign A.
* **Top Performing Channel:** **Email** is the highest-converting channel, generating **$7,909.31** in sales, followed by **Instagram** ($3,873.41) and **Web Banner** ($2,876.88).
* **Channel & Campaign Synergy:** 
  * Campaign B performed significantly better on **Email** ($5.0K vs. $3.0K) and **Web Banner** ($2.1K vs. $0.8K).
  * Campaign A held a slight edge over Campaign B on **Instagram** ($2.2K vs. $1.7K).

## 🛠️ Tools Used
* **Microsoft Excel / Power BI:** Data cleaning, aggregation, and visual dashboarding.
